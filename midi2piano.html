Important

The converter maps MIDI notes to the exact keyboard sequence shown in the game. Notes outside the 62-key range are skipped.

The current PianoModule exposes playKey(), which presses and releases each key individually. Therefore MIDI chords occurring at exactly the same time are emitted as consecutive zero-delay key presses rather than true simultaneous key-down events.
