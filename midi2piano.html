Generated Lua

Important

The converter maps MIDI notes to the exact keyboard sequence shown in the game. Notes outside the 62-key range are skipped.

MIDI note-off events are preserved. The generated Lua passes each note's duration to playKey(key, duration), so long notes are held for their actual MIDI duration instead of being tapped instantly.
